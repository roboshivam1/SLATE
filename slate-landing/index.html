YOUR CURRENT MODEL [ERRONEOUS SCHEMA]

Derivatives of composed functions multiply.

(f ∘ g)' = f' × g'

Treats the inner function as if it were decoupled, evaluating outer rate without reference to internal scale.

CORRECT MENTAL MODEL [CALIBRATED SCHEMATA]

The chain rule composes derivatives.

(f ∘ g)'(x) = f'(g(x)) · g'(x)

The outer function operates at the output of the inner function, transmitting rates through nested composition.
